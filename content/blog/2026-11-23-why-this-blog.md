I build two kinds of things. Automations: voice agents that answer the phone, workflows that move leads between apps, WhatsApp assistants that reply from a business's own knowledge. And AI video: product ads, cinematic shorts and avatar presenters.

Both change fast. A tool that worked last month gets a new version, a model learns a new trick, a client asks for something nobody has built yet. Most of what I learn would disappear into finished projects if I didn't write it down.

So this blog is my working log. Expect short, practical posts:

- **Builds:** how a workflow or agent is put together, node by node.
- **Fixes:** what broke and how I got it running again.
- **Video:** how I plan shots and keep characters and products consistent across a whole ad or film.
- **Business:** what clients actually ask for, and what makes automation worth paying for.
